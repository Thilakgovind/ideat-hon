I am Meena, a fictional athlete representing questions we want to test with women in Tamil Nadu.

I love running. But getting to a trial is not just about training. I need to know the route, the cost, and whether I can get home safely. Sometimes the opportunity itself reaches me too late, buried in messages and different websites.

I also want a place where I can ask about my period, recovery, or how I am feeling, without being judged. I do not want someone deciding for me. I want clear choices and a next step I can trust.

So here is my question: could one companion help me find the right support when I need it?

With Avvai, I can speak in Tamil, Tanglish, or English. I can look for an opportunity, plan my travel, explore funding, and open a private wellbeing conversation. Avvai shows me an option. I decide what to do.

This is our working prototype. Now we need to listen to real athletes, test these assumptions, and improve it with them.
