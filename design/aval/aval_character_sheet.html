AVAL

அவள்

“Aval” means she in Tamil. A Chennai woman who travels the same buses and trains as our users: braid with jasmine, gold jimikki earrings, plum kurta with a pink dupatta, phone always in hand. Friendly first, serious only when it matters.

Each state maps to an app moment: greeting on Home, listening while she speaks, talking during Sarvam voice replies, thinking while agents search, alert during SOS or a missed check-in.

Rules for using AVAL

Colours
Kurta · Plum #4A1D6E
Dupatta · Pink #E0457B
Jimikki & flats · Marigold #F6A623

Where she appears
  • Home: small head in the “Ask AVAL” button
  • AVAL page: full body, live states
  • Live trip: head only, top corner, calm
  • Loading: thinking face
  • SOS: alert face, never cute animations
Do / don't
  • Keep the braid + jasmine: it's her signature
  • Always pair her with text, never icon-only
  • Don't make her joke during SOS
  • Don't change skin tone or body shape per screen
